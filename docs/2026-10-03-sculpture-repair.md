# HA identity and sculpture repair

The October 2 model repeats full panels in tightly packed banks. Their surfaces
intersect and obscure the intended silhouette. The canvas also uses a measured
position outside its content region, so scrolling can separate the sculpture
from its intended space. Journey selection has a separate lifetime in the scene
and the role explorer, allowing their active roles to disagree after navigation.

## Geometry direction

The identity is a compact HA ligature with distinct uprights and a shared,
level copper bridge. Its light, dark, monochrome, and optical favicon variants
are authored from the same geometry in `web/src/components/brandGeometry.ts`.

The sculpture is one continuous beveled ribbon mesh. Every chapter keeps the
same connected vertex topology while it folds into a triangular intro aperture,
a wide work frame, a four-crease career atlas, an inward-folded About frame, or
an open Contact fold. Copper is reserved for the inner bevel and join; pearl
faces and dark teal reverse surfaces keep the structure legible. Journey's
route and selected station follow the ribbon surface. The orthographic camera
fits each form to its stage, including the outgoing form at the destination
stage's aspect ratio during transitions.

## Required verification

Inspect the rendered model, not just the DOM rectangle. Check all chapter poses
and transitions, English and Arabic, desktop, and phones. Scroll the actual
content regions to confirm the object stays attached and clips with its section.
Check route selection, resizing, reduced motion, WebGL loss, and favicon sizes.
Project pages without a scene anchor should not acquire a floating sculpture.

This is a local repair. The production portfolio was deliberately stopped;
publishing or restarting it is a separate action.
